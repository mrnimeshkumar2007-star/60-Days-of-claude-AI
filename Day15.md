## Learn About Vedic Astrology

Vedic Astrology (Jyotish Shastra) is one of the oldest knowledge systems originating from India. It studies the relationship between planetary positions at the time of birth and their symbolic influence on human life.

Unlike popular sun-sign astrology, Vedic astrology considers:

* Ascendant (Lagna)
* Moon Sign (Rashi)
* Nakshatra (Lunar Constellation)
* Planetary Placements
* Vimshottari Dasha
* Yogas and Doshas
* Planetary Transits

Its purpose is not to create fear or dependency but to provide guidance, encourage self-awareness, and support wiser life choices.
