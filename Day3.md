###Role-Based Prompting:
<img width="1254" height="1254" alt="1000223155" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/067162e3-df48-4ca8-a458-9ed1f5b0afa5" />
An AI technique where you explicitly instruct the model to adopt a specific persona, profession, or character. 
### Step 1: Read the resources

Review the materials provided by your instructor or course on Role-Based Prompting.

### Step 2: Choose a question

Example question:

> How should I validate a new SaaS product idea with a limited budget?

### Step 3: Ask Claude without a role

Prompt:

> How should I validate a new SaaS product idea with a limited budget?

Save Claude's response.

### Step 4: Ask Claude using a Founder persona

Prompt:

> Act as an experienced startup founder who has launched multiple SaaS companies. How should I validate a new SaaS product idea with a limited budget?

Save Claude's response.

### Step 5: Ask Claude using a Developer persona

Prompt:

> Act as a senior software developer with experience building SaaS products. How should I validate a new SaaS product idea with a limited budget?

Save Claude's response.

### Step 6: Compare the responses

A typical comparison might look like this:

| Aspect        | No Role                    | Founder Persona                                  | Developer Persona                             |
| ------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Focus         | General advice             | Market validation, customers, revenue            | Technical feasibility, MVP development        |
| Tone          | Neutral                    | Business-oriented                                | Technical and implementation-focused          |
| Priorities    | Broad recommendations      | Customer interviews, pricing, product-market fit | Architecture, prototyping, technology choices |
| Metrics       | General success indicators | Acquisition, retention, revenue                  | Performance, scalability, development effort  |
| Actionability | Moderate                   | Business action plan                             | Technical action plan                         |

### Expected Insight

Role-based prompting changes the perspective from which the model answers:

* **No role** → balanced, generic response.
* **Founder role** → emphasizes customers, growth, competition, revenue, and business risk.
* **Developer role** → emphasizes implementation, architecture, technical constraints, and MVP execution.
