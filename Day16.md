# Day 16-Build Your First Stock Research Skill

## 🎯 Objective

To explore how a well-structured AI prompt can transform ChatGPT from a simple chatbot into a powerful learning and research assistant capable of performing:

* 📊 Mega Cap Stock Snapshots
* 🔍 Fundamental Deep Dives
* ⚖️ Company Comparisons
* 🧠 Business and Investment Analysis
* 📚 Educational Insights for Long-Term Learning

The objective was not to predict stock prices, but to understand **how great businesses create value and how prompt engineering can enhance critical thinking and decision-making.**

---

## 📖 Learn About the Prompt

This prompt was designed using a **framework-based approach** rather than asking a simple question.

Instead of:

> "Tell me about Apple stock."

It instructed AI to:

* Use live fundamental data.
* Analyze business quality.
* Compare companies objectively.
* Identify strengths and risks.
* Explain concepts in simple language.
* Avoid investment recommendations.

The prompt converted AI into a:

* Fundamental Analyst
* Business Researcher
* Comparison Engine
* Educational Mentor
