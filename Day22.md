## Key Learnings

### Market Learning

* Regulatory compliance is a significant challenge for Indian manufacturing SMEs.
* Compliance requirements vary across states and change frequently.
* Most businesses still rely on manual processes and consultants.

### Customer Learning

* Factory owners, CFOs, and HR/Legal heads experience audit anxiety and compliance uncertainty.
* Customers are willing to pay for solutions that reduce penalties and save time.
* Hindi and localized support can be a strong differentiator in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities.

### Product Learning

* Automation of compliance workflows creates immediate value.
* Real-time alerts and regulatory intelligence are highly demanded features.
* Integration with government portals is critical for adoption.

### Business Learning

* Compliance is a recurring problem, creating strong SaaS subscription potential.
* High customer retention is possible due to the mission-critical nature of compliance.
* Early validation indicates strong problem-solution fit.
