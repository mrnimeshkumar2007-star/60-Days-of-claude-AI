# MVP Recommendation

## What Should Be Built First?

### Core MVP

1. Supplier Data Collection Portal
2. Automated Supplier Outreach
3. Scope 3 Emissions Calculation Engine
4. Emissions Dashboard
5. Compliance Reporting Export
6. Basic AI Recommendations for Reduction Opportunities

### MVP Goal

Reduce carbon data collection effort by at least 50%.

---

## What Should NOT Be Built?

* Full ESG suite
* Advanced forecasting
* Carbon offset marketplace
* Supplier benchmarking network
* Complex scenario simulation
* Multi-region regulatory engine
* Enterprise workflow automation

These increase complexity without validating core demand.

# Final Verdict

## 🟢 Strong Demand Signal

### Why?

✅ Pain point is severe and growing.

✅ Regulatory pressure creates urgency.

✅ Identified budget-owning buyers.

✅ 30+ customer interviews completed.

✅ 25-company beta waitlist demonstrates market interest.

✅ Pilot execution provides early proof of value.

### Critical Next Validation Question

Before scaling product development, validate:

**"Will customers pay €15k–€40k annually for automated Scope 3 data collection and reporting today?"**

Achieving 3–5 paid pilot agreements would significantly increase confidence in product-market fit and move the startup from validated demand toward a repeatable enterprise SaaS business.



🔑 **Key Learnings**

1. Scope 3 emissions remain the largest and most difficult sustainability challenge for enterprises.
2. Most companies still depend on spreadsheets and manual supplier outreach.
3. Regulatory pressure is creating urgency to adopt digital carbon-accounting solutions.
4. Sustainability leaders prioritize compliance and reporting efficiency over advanced analytics.
5. Supplier participation and data quality are the biggest barriers to success.
6. Customers value automation and actionable insights more than complex features.
7. Early validation signals are strong:

   * 30+ customer interviews
   * 25-company beta waitlist
   * Successful pilot program
